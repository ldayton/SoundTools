Original WAVs converted losslessly to FLAC and named by their verified MIDI key.
For MIDI 40, take susvib_E1_v1_2.wav; other keys use take v1_1.
Playback adaptations: normalization, soft high-shelf EQ, envelope fades,
crossfaded sustain loops, and continuous digital pitch shifts.
These are sustained ensemble recordings, not recorded portamento transitions.
