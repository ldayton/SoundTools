DoReGoAccidentals.woff2 is a two-glyph subset of
[Bravura](https://github.com/steinbergmedia/bravura/blob/master/redist/otf/Bravura.otf)
by Steinberg, licensed under the SIL Open Font License in OFL.txt.

The approved shapes are the slashed flat, SMuFL U+E442 (accidentalBakiyeFlat),
and the single-stem half-sharp, U+E282 (accidentalQuarterToneSharpStein).
Standalone SVG outlines are included here for inspection.
They are mapped to semantic Unicode U+1D133 and U+1D132 respectively in the
bundled font. In DoReGo, they signify pitches 50 cents below or above a natural.
The modified font is renamed DoReGo Accidentals and has no other glyph coverage.

The outlines retain their proportions while being scaled to a 700-unit cap
height and aligned to the text baseline for readable inline phone labels.
