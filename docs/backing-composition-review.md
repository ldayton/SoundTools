# Composition review: harmony and genre idiom

Reviewed on 20 November 2026. The user chose to **keep tonic vamps for scale
practice**. These remain recognizable genre accompaniments over the selected
practice tonic, not full song arrangements. In particular, twelve-bar phrasing
in the Blues preset does not mean an I–IV–V blues progression. Gospel's chord
movement is within tonic colours, not a claim to reproduce a complete church
reharmonization. No new harmony selector is introduced.

## Findings and changes

Some neighbour figures also landed away from their starting chord tone.                                 | Choose root/third/neighbour together in a reachable hand position. Major 3–2–3 and minor ♭3–4–♭3 figures return by step; the thumb keeps its independent quarter-note pulse.                                                                                                     |
| Shuffle Blues      | An arbitrary inverted lead grip could put the tonic on top, causing “up” and “down” replies to collapse into the same contour.                                                                                                         | Choose a playable root-position lead triad as a group. Root–third–fifth ascends, and fifth–third–root descends, in a register above the two-string rhythm riff.                                                                                                                  |

Added ninths and unresolved colours are not inherently undesirable. The choices
above aim for clearer register, deliberate tension/release and predictable
phrase endings in this particular practice accompaniment.

## Research and application

These are primary instructional sources or firsthand practitioner guidance. The
musical cells are our own; no lesson scores, songs or recordings are copied.

- [Berklee: Piano Voicing Techniques](https://online.berklee.edu/takenote/basic-piano-voicing-techniques/), a lesson excerpt by Paul Schmeling. Use common-tone connection and inversions to control movement and register; place tensions with care. This informed Gospel's legato realization and Soul's more open voicings. Its guidance is not treated as a rule that all close jazz voicings are wrong.
- [Yamaha: How to Play Electric Piano, Clavinet and Organ Sounds](https://hub.yamaha.com/keyboards/k-how-to/how-to-play-electric-piano-clavinet-and-organ-sounds/), by Jerry Kovarsky. Electric piano benefits from clearer, simpler voicings; sustained organ calls for space and connected fingering, particularly alongside a bassist. Applied to Soul's spacing and Gospel's held common tones without adding a blanket sustain pedal.
- [Davell Crawford: Leading Tones in Gospel](https://www.pianogroove.com/blues-piano-lessons/leading-tones-in-gospel/) and [Gospel Accompaniment](https://www.pianogroove.com/blues-piano-lessons/accompanying-gospel-singer/). Moving voices can guide the listener toward the next harmony. We apply that principle to a short suspended-to-triad answer over the tonic, while deliberately omitting the course's chromatic passing-chord progressions from scale practice.
- [Fender: Travis Picking](https://www.fender.com/articles/techniques/travis-picking-on-guitar). Alternating thumb bass supports separate treble notes, syncopation and occasional pinches. This supports retaining the approved picking texture while fixing the actual treble contour, rather than varying the thumb randomly.
- [Cathy Fink: Boom-Chuck Strumming](https://acousticguitar.com/how-to-boomchuck/). Separate bass notes on strong beats and upper-string brushes provide the basic accompaniment. This supports Country's existing division of roles and the distinction between its short strokes and broader strummed Folk.
- [Fender: Blues Guitar Scale and Shuffle](https://www.fender.com/articles/scales/blues-guitar-scale). Two-string shuffle shapes and triplet-based rhythm support the Blues preset's riff. The tonic-only version retains that vocabulary without claiming the lesson's full twelve-bar chord progression.
- [Berklee/Larry Finn: Funk and Rock Drum Rhythms](https://online.berklee.edu/takenote/basic-funk-for-drums/). Genre feel depends on the ensemble, especially the bass subdivision and relative dynamics, not just the drum pattern. The existing even-eighth Rock and lighter sixteenth-based Funk arrangements already express this distinction.
- [Fender: Reggae Curriculum](https://www.fender.com/articles/fender-performances/fender-play-live-reggae-playlist) and [Sound On Sound: Dub Mixing](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/dub-mixing). Short chord chops, a prominent repeating bass part and a complementary organ bubble support the intended roots-reggae texture. We retain that division of labour, rather than filling every space with a lead.
- [StudyBass/Andrew Pouska: Chord Tones in Basslines](https://www.studybass.com/lessons/harmony/chord-tones-in-basslines/). Strong chord-tone placement defines the low-end harmony; roots and fifths are a dependable foundation. Our tonic bass anchors remain, with passing activity confined to written response windows.

## A related realization fix

The ensemble timing pass previously checked held authored tones against the old
progression's chord formula. A historical minor half-diminished chord could
therefore cut a tonic fifth even though the actual score remained on tonic.
Authored tonic scores now use their actual root/quality for that check. This
keeps the new Gospel common-tone legato intact while respecting written rests.
The reference arranger retains its original progression logic.

## Verification

New harmonic regressions cover open added-ninth placement, suspended answers
landing on a complete triad, common-tone legato without filling reply windows,
open Soul spacing, actual stepwise Folk figures and distinct rising/falling Blues
responses. Existing checks still cover every key, both qualities and feels,
physical string reach, native sample coverage, timing, loop bounds and selected
scale vocabulary. Rendered audio validates levels and technical behavior; it
cannot prove a subjective preference for a composition.

Fresh WAVs and before/after material are kept under ignored
`work/composition-review/`. Only the affected compositions require full-program
remeasurement; unchanged presets retain their prior measured factory mixes.
